Top Tourist Attractions in Naran, Pakistan

Naran is a popular tourist destination nestled in the Kaghan Valley. Known for its alpine beauty, glacial lakes, and scenic mountain trails, it offers a perfect retreat into nature.

Dudipatsar Lake

This "Queen of Lakes" is known for its icy blue water and green meadows. A remote trekking gem for nature lovers.

Babusar Top

At 13,700 ft above sea level, Babusar Top offers panoramic views of snowy peaks and connects Kaghan Valley with Gilgit-Baltistan.

Jalkhad 2

Another viewpoint near Jalkhad, popular for its scenic camping areas, streams, and stunning green landscapes.

Kiwai Waterfall

A beautiful waterfall on the way to Naran, often used as a refreshing rest spot with natural beauty and roadside dhabas.

Lalazar Meadows

Known for its lush green slopes, wildflowers, and views of snow-covered peaks, Lalazar is a high-altitude paradise near Naran.

Lulusar Lake

One of the largest lakes in Kaghan Valley, Lulusar is a sparkling body of water surrounded by majestic mountains and untouched nature.

Naran Valley

The main town itself is a hub of activity with markets, hotels, eateries, and serves as the basecamp for nearby attractions.

Saral Lake

Accessible via a rugged trek, Saral Lake is a hidden alpine gem with tranquil surroundings and glacial charm.

Saiful Malook Lake (Close-up)

This closer view highlights the crystal-clear waters, mythical ambiance, and captivating reflections of Malika Parbat.

Sharan Forest

A serene pine forest known for eco-camping, wooden huts, and peaceful walks amid chirping birds and forest mist.

Sogran Valley

A lesser-known hidden valley featuring lush hills, wooden cabins, and a tranquil escape from the crowded tourist zones.

Visit Naran: From tranquil lakes to majestic mountains — every corner of Naran is a story waiting to be explored!
