Top Tourist Attractions in Quetta, Pakistan

Quetta, the capital of Balochistan, is surrounded by mountains and filled with cultural treasures, natural beauty, and a unique local vibe that reflects centuries of history.

Hanna Lake

A serene lake nestled between rocky hills, Hanna Lake offers paddle boating, peaceful scenery, and a chance to escape into nature. Ideal for family picnics and photography.

Hazarganji Chiltan National Park

This park is a sanctuary for endangered species like the Chiltan ibex and features rocky trails and rare flora. It's perfect for nature lovers, hikers, and wildlife photographers.

Ziarat Juniper Forest

Home to centuries-old juniper trees, this ancient forest is one of the largest of its kind. It offers crisp air, mountain peace, and an unforgettable walk among timeless trees.

Mariabad

A vibrant neighborhood of Quetta known for its colorful homes and Hazara community. It's full of character, history, and street culture, offering a window into local life.

Pari Chashma

A hidden gem with natural springs and lush greenery in the hills of Ziarat. Local legends add a mystical feel to this peaceful picnic spot surrounded by nature.

Pechi Dam

Located in the outskirts of Quetta, Pechi Dam is a calm reservoir popular for short fishing trips, photography, and peaceful weekend getaways.

Pir Ghaib Waterfalls

This enchanting site features freshwater falls, palm trees, and natural pools. The place is steeped in legend and is perfect for adventure and spiritual seekers.

Quaid-e-Azam Residency

Located in Ziarat, this beautiful colonial-era building is where Muhammad Ali Jinnah spent his final days. It stands as a national heritage site surrounded by scenic beauty.

Urak Valley

A green haven just outside Quetta, Urak Valley is famous for apple orchards, waterfalls, and breathtaking landscapes, especially beautiful in spring and early summer.
