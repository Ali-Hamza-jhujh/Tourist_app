Top Tourist Attractions in Multan, Pakistan

Multan, the "City of Saints," is one of the oldest cities in South Asia. It's known for its rich heritage, vibrant bazaars, and stunning Islamic architecture.

Ahmad Shah Abdali Memorial

Honoring the founder of the Durrani Empire, this memorial celebrates his historic visit to Multan and its legacy.

Band River

A picturesque riverside spot just outside Multan, perfect for evening strolls and enjoying calm waterscapes.

Baudīn Zikris Shrine

A lesser-known sacred site visited by those seeking spiritual solace and a glimpse into local faith traditions.

Char Minar

An architectural tribute reminiscent of Delhi's Charminar, this elegant monument adds a cultural touch to the Multan skyline.

Delhi Gate

One of the historic gateways into the old city, offering a walk through Multan’s architectural past.

Dolat Gate

Another storied gate of Multan’s walled city, showcasing how defenses and culture merged in bygone eras.

Haram Gate

A historic entrance that once welcomed pilgrims and traders — rich in stories of the city’s past.

Jain Mandir

A heritage temple reflecting the diverse religious history of Multan, beloved by heritage and architecture enthusiasts.

Khunu Burj

A watchtower offering panoramic views over old Multan — a serene point to reflect on the city below.

Invasion Memorial

A monument commemorating key historical battles that shaped the city's identity and resilience.

City Viewpoint

An observatory point that gives sweeping views of Multan — ideal for avid photographers.

Mazar of Shah Rukn‑e‑Kan

A spiritual mausoleum built around an ancient saintly figure — reflective and peaceful for contemplative visitors.

Monument Van Alexander

A newer cultural landmark dedicated to a notable contributor in Multan’s modern history and arts.

Multan Arts Council

Hub of cultural performances, art exhibitions, and theater reflecting the creative pulse of the city.

Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower)

A prominent city landmark perfect for the clock watchers and architectural buffs exploring central Multan.

Nigar Khan Shrine / Mai Maharban

Dedicated space honoring the spiritual legacy of Nigar Khan and Mai Maharban — known for blessings and local reverence.

Shrine of Shah Shams

A revered religious site where devotees come to pay respects to the saint and find inner peace.

Shrine of Shah Sharyousuf

A sacred, serene sanctuary with spiritual ambiance and architectural beauty.

Tomb of Khawaja

An ancient and peaceful resting place, rich in heritage and local legends.

Tomb of Shah

An artistically designed mausoleum honoring an influential saintly figure of Multan’s past.

Visit Multan: A timeless blend of spiritual history, vibrant culture, and mesmerizing architecture awaits you in this ancient city!
