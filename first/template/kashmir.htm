Top Tourist Attractions in Kashmir, Pakistan

Kashmir is a breathtaking region filled with stunning landscapes, lush valleys, crystal-clear lakes, and rich cultural heritage. A true paradise on earth!

Banjosa Lake

A man-made lake surrounded by pine forests and hills, Banjosa is perfect for a peaceful boat ride and family picnics.

Canoeing - Neelum Valley

Adventure seekers can enjoy canoeing in the crystal-clear rivers of Neelum Valley, offering thrilling views and refreshing waters.

Jhelum Valley

This valley is lined with the Jhelum River and picturesque towns. It's known for its panoramic views and rich cultural vibe.

Lepa Valley

Lepa Valley, also spelled Leepa, features terraced fields, wooden houses, and a strong local culture. It's visually stunning in autumn.

Pir Chinasi

Located 30 km from Muzaffarabad, this sacred shrine and viewpoint offers majestic views of snow-capped mountains and valleys.

Ramkot Fort

Built on the confluence of River Jhelum and Mangla Lake, this 16th-century fort is an architectural gem and a hidden historic treasure.

Red Fort

Also known as Muzaffarabad Fort, it was once a strategic military structure. Its historic ruins overlook the Neelum and Jhelum rivers.

Shounter Lake

Located in Shounter Valley, this glacial lake is surrounded by wildflowers and snow-capped peaks, offering raw, untouched beauty.

Toli Peer

A slightly different viewpoint than Toli Pir, this peak allows panoramic views and is ideal for photography and hiking.

Visit Kashmir: A wonderland of nature, history, and culture awaits. Experience the serenity of northern Pakistan!
