Top Tourist Attractions in Bahawalpur, Pakistan

Bahawalpur is a historical city in southern Punjab known for its palaces, forts, deserts, and the royal heritage of the Nawabs.

Noor Mahal

A stunning palace built in the Italian style by the Nawab of Bahawalpur. It now serves as a museum with well-preserved royal relics.

Derawar Fort

A majestic desert fort in the Cholistan Desert, with massive walls and deep historical roots dating back centuries.

Central Library Bahawalpur

One of Pakistan’s oldest public libraries, founded in 1924, known for its colonial architecture and vast literary collections.

Lal Suhanra National Park

A biosphere reserve with forests, desert, and wetlands — perfect for wildlife spotting, bird watching, and peaceful walks in nature.

Gulzar Mahal

A lesser-known but beautiful palace built during the reign of Nawab Sadiq Muhammad Khan. Features stunning Indo-European design.

Abbasi Mosque

Located near Derawar Fort, this white marble mosque reflects Mughal architectural influences and is a serene place of worship with elegant domes and minarets.

Bahawalpur Museum

Showcases archaeological, Islamic, and regional exhibits including artwork, manuscripts, and artifacts from the Bahawalpur State era.

Cholistan Desert

Stretching over 25,000 square kilometers, this desert offers jeep rallies, camel rides, desert camping, and a glimpse into the nomadic lifestyle.

Darbar Mahal

A red-brick royal palace with striking Indo-Saracenic architecture, built in the early 20th century. Now under army administration but still admired from the outside.

SS World Family Park

A modern recreational park with rides, attractions, food courts, and entertainment — perfect for families and children.

Uch Sharif

One of the oldest cities in South Asia, Uch Sharif is renowned for its ancient shrines and tombs built in Central Asian and Islamic architectural styles.

Visit Bahawalpur: A royal city offering architectural wonders, desert adventures, and a unique cultural heritage.
