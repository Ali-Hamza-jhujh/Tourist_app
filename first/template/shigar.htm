Top Tourist Attractions in Shigar, Gilgit-Baltistan

Shigar is a serene valley in Baltistan offering a beautiful mix of culture, ancient architecture, and stunning landscapes at the gateway to the Karakoram mountains.

Shigar Fort (Fong-Khar)

A 400-year-old Tibetan-style fort beautifully restored by the Aga Khan Trust. Now a museum and heritage hotel, it offers a deep dive into Balti culture.

Ambrique Mosque

One of the oldest mosques in the region, this wooden structure showcases unique Tibetan-Islamic architecture and spiritual tranquility.

Shigar Valley

Known for its lush greenery, peaceful rivers, and majestic mountain views. The valley is perfect for relaxing, hiking, and exploring traditional Balti villages.

Katpana Cold Desert

Located between Shigar and Skardu, this high-altitude desert offers surreal white sand dunes with snowy peaks in the backdrop — a rare natural wonder.

Kharphocho Fort

Though technically in nearby Skardu, this fort can be visited during a Shigar trip. It offers panoramic views of the entire region from a hilltop.

Chutron Hot Springs

Chutron Hot Springs in Shigar is a hidden therapeutic gem, known for its warm mineral waters enclosed within a stone structure. Locals and visitors alike enjoy soaking their feet or bathing in these natural hot springs, believed to have healing properties. The atmosphere is peaceful, traditional, and deeply refreshing — a perfect stop to relax after exploring the valleys and mountains of Baltistan.

Blind Lake (Sigar)

Located between Skardu and Shigar, Blind Lake (also known as Sigar Lake) is a breathtaking freshwater lake surrounded by towering mountains. Its serene, glassy surface reflects the sky and peaks, offering a tranquil escape for travelers. The lake is nourished by the Indus River and serves as a water reservoir for nearby areas. It's an ideal spot for picnics, drone photography, and peaceful walks amid nature.
