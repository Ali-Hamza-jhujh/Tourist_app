Explore Skardu – Hidden Gem of Pakistan

Skardu offers a magical blend of majestic mountains, serene lakes, and centuries-old culture. Discover the top places that make Skardu a paradise for nature lovers, trekkers, and explorers.

Deosai National Park

Known as the “Land of Giants,” Deosai is one of the highest plateaus in the world. The park is home to rare wildlife, lush meadows, and breathtaking views, making it a must-visit destination for nature lovers and adventurers alike.

K2 Base Camp

For thrill-seekers and trekkers, the journey to the base of the world’s second-highest mountain, K2, is a life-changing experience. The trail winds through glaciers, rivers, and majestic mountain landscapes.

Kachura Lake

Located near Skardu, Kachura Lake is divided into Upper and Lower parts. It’s famous for its crystal-clear waters, trout fish, and the peaceful ambiance perfect for boating and relaxation.

Kharphocho Fort

Perched on a cliff, this ancient fort offers panoramic views of Skardu town and the confluence of the Indus and Shigar Rivers. It's a historical site with roots going back to the 16th century.

Manthal Buddha Rock

This ancient Buddhist carving dates back to the 8th century and is a symbol of Skardu’s historical connections to Buddhism before the arrival of Islam. It’s a cultural heritage site surrounded by mountains.

Manthokha Waterfall

One of the most stunning waterfalls in Pakistan, Manthokha Waterfall is located in Kharmang Valley. The 180-feet high waterfall is surrounded by green pastures, grazing yaks, and peaceful mountain vibes.

Shangrila Resort

Also known as “Heaven on Earth,” this iconic resort features a heart-shaped lake, luxurious stay, and surreal mountain scenery. It's a favorite among honeymooners and luxury tourists.

Satpara Lake

Satpara Lake is a serene glacial lake near Skardu, supplying water to the entire town. Surrounded by hills and ideal for boating, the lake also provides a peaceful escape from urban life.

Sheosar Lake

Located in Deosai Plains, Sheosar Lake is one of the most beautiful high-altitude lakes in the world. On a clear day, it reflects the snowy peak of Nanga Parbat, creating postcard-perfect views.

Skardu Baltistan Culture

Baltistan is rich in Tibetan-influenced traditions, food, music, and hospitality. Explore the local culture through its traditional dress, wooden houses, apricot orchards, and warm-hearted people.

Katpana Cold Desert

Known as the Cold Desert of Skardu, this surreal landscape features sand dunes covered with snow in winter. It’s one of the highest deserts in the world, offering a unique blend of terrain.

Visit Skardu to witness the magic of Pakistan’s northern frontier where nature, culture, and adventure collide!
