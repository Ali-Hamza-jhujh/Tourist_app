Top Tourist Attractions in Gilgit, Pakistan

Gilgit is a gateway to some of Pakistan’s most breathtaking mountain ranges and valleys. Rich in culture and surrounded by towering peaks, it's a dream destination for nature lovers and adventurers.

Karakoram Highway

Often referred to as the Eighth Wonder of the World, this highway passes through Gilgit and offers incredible views of the Karakoram Range.

Barino Village

A hidden gem with natural streams and wooden houses surrounded by peaceful pastures, perfect for serene exploration.

Bugrat Valley

A stunning green valley surrounded by rugged mountains and ideal for camping, hiking, and nature walks.

Junction of Three Mountain Ranges

A spectacular point near Gilgit where the Karakoram, Himalayas, and Hindu Kush mountain ranges meet.

Chinese Graveyard

A historic memorial site honoring the Chinese workers who lost their lives during the construction of the Karakoram Highway.

Firoza Lake

A mesmerizing turquoise-colored lake nestled in the mountains, known for its reflection and peaceful surroundings.

Kargah Buddha

An ancient rock carving of Buddha dating back to the 7th century, carved into a cliff face near Gilgit.

Kutwal Lake

Located in Haramosh Valley, this high-altitude lake is surrounded by glaciers and dramatic peaks, perfect for trekkers.

Nalter Valley (Winter View)

A snowy paradise in winter offering skiing, frozen lakes, and surreal landscapes that attract thrill-seekers and photographers.

Sherqilla (Starngiwal)

A beautiful village with orchards and traditional homes along the Ghizer River — great for cultural experiences.

Tagmughal Valley

A lesser-known paradise offering untouched beauty, vibrant meadows, and panoramic mountain views away from the crowd.

Visit Gilgit: From ancient forts to alpine lakes and world-class mountain views — Gilgit is where history and nature meet!
