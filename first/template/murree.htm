Top Tourist Attractions in Murree, Pakistan

Murree, the "Queen of the Hills," is a stunning hill station nestled in the Pir Panjal range. Famous for its lush greenery, cool climate, and scenic beauty.

Mall Road

The bustling center of Murree, full of local shops, food stalls, and vibrant nightlife. A favorite place for evening walks and shopping.

Pindi Point

Offers panoramic views of Rawalpindi city. A popular spot for chair lift rides and scenic photography.

Kashmir Point

Peaceful and serene, this viewpoint provides an incredible view of snow-covered mountains during winter.

Patriata (New Murree)

Famous for its chair lifts and cable cars between lush pine forests. A must-visit for adventure lovers.

Bhurban

A luxurious escape near Murree, known for its five-star resorts, golf course, and peaceful surroundings.

Ayubia National Park

Home to dense forests, diverse wildlife, and the famous "Pipeline Walking Track" between Ayubia and Dunga Gali. A haven for nature lovers.

Ghora Gali

Known for its colonial-era charm and beautiful views, Ghora Gali offers hiking spots and quiet guesthouses for a peaceful retreat.

Nathiagali

A cool, forested town with panoramic views and hiking trails like Mukshpuri and Miranjani — perfect for outdoor enthusiasts.

Rawat

Located between Islamabad and Murree, Rawat is a peaceful village known for its green fields, fresh air, and rural charm.

Upper Topa

Situated at a higher altitude, Upper Topa is cooler and less crowded — ideal for snow lovers and those looking to escape the tourist buzz.

Visit Murree: Refresh your soul with cool breezes, tall pine trees, and mountain magic – just a few hours from Islamabad!
